Strategic Roadmap: The Policy Agenda

Sir/Ma'am, addressing the current state requires a targeted, phased approach. Our recommendations focus on immediate high-impact interventions, structural reforms, and a long-term vision for digital sovereignty and leadership.

Phase 1 — Quick Wins
  1. Emergency RPKI Mandate: Issue immediate regulatory mandates for all ISPs with IPv6 prefixes to implement RPKI ROAs within 6 months. Prioritize MT-MPLS, ASMedi, and IAM-AS.
  2. Direct ISP Bottleneck Engagement: Convene immediate, high-level meetings with leadership of MT-MPLS and ASMedi to establish binding IPv6 adoption targets and timelines, backed by incentives or penalties.
  3. CPE/Router Import Standards: Implement new regulatory standards requiring all imported or distributed customer-premises equipment (CPE/routers) to be IPv6-enabled by default.
  4. Government IPv6-First Procurement: Issue a directive mandating that all new government digital services and IT procurements must be IPv6-only or IPv6-first, and migrate critical existing services to IPv6 within 12 months.
Phase 2 — Structural
  1. IXP IPv6 Mandate: Enforce a regulatory requirement for domestic IXPs (FEZIX, CAS-IX) to deploy and facilitate IPv6 LAN peering within 18 months, offering technical assistance if needed.
  2. National IPv6 Deployment Task Force: Establish a dedicated inter-ministerial task force with ISP, regulator, and technical community representation to oversee national IPv6 transition, set KPIs, and monitor progress.
  3. Capacity Building & Training Programs: Launch national programs to train network engineers, IT professionals, and government staff on IPv6 deployment, management, and security.
  4. Financial Sector IPv6 Mandate: Issue a directive requiring financial institutions (especially those with 0% readiness like the Banking sector) to develop and implement IPv6 transition plans.
  5. Review and Update Telecoms Policy: Modernize the national telecommunications regulatory framework to explicitly prioritize and incentivize IPv6 adoption across all market segments.
Phase 3 — Vision
  1. Digital Sovereignty Initiative: Develop a long-term strategy to reduce reliance on external CDNs for core web services by strengthening domestic hosting and IPv6 infrastructure.
  2. Education & Innovation Hubs: Establish academic and industry partnerships to foster IPv6 research, development, and innovation, positioning the nation as a regional leader in next-generation internet technologies.
  3. IPv6-Only Public Infrastructure: Explore and pilot IPv6-only deployments for specific non-critical public services, laying the groundwork for a future IPv6-centric network environment.
  4. Regional Internet Governance Leadership: Actively participate in and advocate for IPv6 adoption within regional bodies, sharing best practices and fostering a more resilient African internet.
